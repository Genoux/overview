Five parks for the hub

Each is laid out by hand from the same isometric tileset, in its own colours. Water, rocks, logs and bushes are solid: blobs and the animals walk around them. Every bridge reaches every open cell (the tests check it). Open one in the map to see it lived in.

Tiles: the "isometric tileset" pack from your Downloads (author and license to confirm). Source of the layouts: js/hubmaps.js.
