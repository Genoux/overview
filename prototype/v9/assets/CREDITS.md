# Art credits

The hub's ground, plants, rocks and the two wild animals come from two pixel-art packs the owner downloaded:

- `tiles/`: picked from **isometric tileset** (`isometric tileset.zip`, 32×32 isometric tiles): the tiles the five hub compositions in `js/hubmaps.js` use.
- `critters/`: picked from **critters** (`critters.zip`): the stag and the badger, idle and walk, four directions.

Author, store page and license: **to confirm before this leaves the owner's machines or is shown to anyone.** Most itch.io packs of this kind require credit; add the author's name and link here once known.

Everything else on the map (islands, plots, blobs, labels) is drawn in code.
